# Finance Defense
## Anti-Scam Alerts

---

## Objectives
- Reduce the risk of financial scams
- Detect warning signals in real time
- Guide users toward safe actions

---

## Most Common Scam Types
- Phishing via email or SMS (fake banking communications)
- Fake support operators using urgent requests
- Links to cloned pages to steal credentials
- Requests for wire transfers or unverified payments

---

## Key Alerts to Display
- Login from an unusual device or location
- Multiple failed login attempts
- New beneficiary combined with a high amount
- Sudden password change or account recovery method updates

---

## Alert Response Flow
1. Anomaly detection
2. Transaction block or verification
3. Immediate user notification
4. Identity confirmation (MFA / challenge)
5. Escalation to the anti-fraud team

---

## Recommended User Messages
- "We detected unusual activity on your account."
- "Do not share OTP codes or passwords with anyone."
- "Confirm actions only through the official app or verified channels."
- "If you do not recognize this operation, block it immediately and contact support."

---

## Effectiveness KPIs
- Rate of alerts confirmed as fraud
- Average incident response time
- Reduction in scam-related financial losses
- False-positive rate and UX impact

---
